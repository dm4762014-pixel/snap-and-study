# Snap & Study — work sample

**Purpose:** help a student move from a confusing question/photo through hints,
follow-up explanations, one-question practice and a saved revision record.

**Current work:** optional Telegram, bounded sessions, retry/discard controls,
readable recaps and a complete study-session download. The download does not call
AI and omits onboarding details and photo files; typed content still needs review.

**Verified:** 16 automated tests with mocked providers. A separate real browser
flow covered a photo, hint, contextual follow-up, wrong/correct quiz answers and
a recap download. The new full-session download was also checked in the browser.
See `STUDY-FLOW-RESULTS.md` and the curated `artifacts/` files.

**Contribution:** substantial implementation, debugging and recorded testing were
done with Codex. The applicant should explain their actual decisions, personal
usage and learning in their own words. Do not claim independent authorship or
student adoption from this scripted test.

**Limits:** answers can be wrong; no learning-outcome study, broad accuracy test,
real student trial or current public deployment is established. No real Telegram
message was sent during these checks. API credentials are private and excluded.

**Next personal step:** use one real study question, explain how the hint changed
your approach, and get permission for a trial with one other student.
