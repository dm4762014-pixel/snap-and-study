Revision Recap: Adding Fractions with Unlike Denominators

Topics Covered:
Adding fractions with different denominators and converting fractions to equivalent forms.

Misunderstandings Corrected:
Adding both top and bottom numbers directly (for example, 2/3 + 1/6 = 3/9 or 1/4 + 1/2 = 2/6) is incorrect. The denominator defines the size of each fractional part, so adding denominators changes the size rather than counting the pieces.

Key Explanations and Formulas:
To add fractions, you must first find a common denominator so the pieces are equal in size. Once the denominators match, add only the numerators and keep the common denominator constant: a/c + b/c = (a + b)/c.

Distinction Between Hints and Solved Answers:
A hint prompts you to find equivalent fractions with matching bottom numbers without giving the final sum. Fully solved answers show the step-by-step conversion and addition, such as changing 2/3 + 1/6 into 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6, or changing 1/4 + 1/2 into 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4.
