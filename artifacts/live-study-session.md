## Your question

Explain why 1/4 + 1/2 = 3/4 in two short sentences.

## AI response

To add these fractions, we first rewrite $\frac{1}{2}$ as the equivalent fraction $\frac{2}{4}$ so both numbers share a common denominator. Adding $\frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{2}{4}$ simply combines the top numbers ($1 + 2$), giving a total of $\frac{3}{4}$.

**Key Takeaway:** Before adding fractions, convert them to equivalent fractions with matching denominators so you can simply add the top numbers.
